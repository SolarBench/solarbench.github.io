# Citation Guide

SolarBench harmonizes existing open datasets and new data contributions collected by research groups and organizations. Collecting, processing, and harmonizing these datasets requires substantial effort from the contributors. To recognize these contributions, we encourage users of SolarBench to cite both SolarBench and the original data sources for the datasets they use. An example wording would be: “We accessed the X and Y datasets through SolarBench.” Please refer to the citation guidelines below.

## SolarBench

If you find SolarBench useful, please cite our paper:
